ℹ️ About this network

Each station has its own detailed page that updates every hour: an X-band wave radar on Ram Island (an hourly 5-minute movie of the sea surface, boats and breaking waves), two Sofar Spotter wave buoys (waves, wind estimate, pressure, and Smart Mooring sensors) four BlueSonde water-quality buoys (temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen, turbidity, and chlorophyll or tryptophan), two weather stations (Ram Island on the coast and the 363 Acre Woods inland, which also measures soil temperature and moisture), and two marsh and pond stations (Biddeford Pool salt marsh and Great Pond). Every page has a "What do these numbers mean?" guide. The complete raw archive is on UNE Box. This page reads the latest numbers straight from those dashboards each time it loads and refreshes itself every 10 minutes.

    Wave data: Sofar Ocean. Water-quality data: BlueSonde via CUMULUS (Coastal Measures). Tide predictions: NOAA. Built and maintained by the University of New England.
